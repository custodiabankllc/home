FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I open an account?

Choose Open Account, enter your details, and set your PIN. You can open an individual account or a joint account with another holder.

How does the debit card work?

Apply for your Mastercard debit card from the Cards page of your dashboard. Your card details are created once, and the card is activated after approval. Card details stay hidden until you choose to reveal them.

What happens when I send money in another currency?

Transfers between Custodia accounts in different currencies are converted at the current exchange rate. You are debited in your currency and the recipient is credited in theirs, and both transaction records show the amounts.

What are client tiers?

There are four tiers, from Bronze to Platinum Elite. Everyone starts at the first tier and can apply to upgrade. Higher tiers have higher daily transfer limits, and Platinum Elite is unlimited.

Can more than one person sign on an account?

Yes. Joint accounts support a main signatory plus additional signatories, each signing in with their own credentials, so every action is clearly attributed.
